01MISSION LOG

A flight system that shows its work.

Solaris X-450 asks a practical question: what changes when a small electric aircraft can observe, route, and learn from the energy around it?

05WHY SOLAR

Not a promise of free flight. A better question.

Solar assistance is interesting because it makes the energy budget visible. It invites a student, sponsor, or judge to look at the whole chain—from irradiance to motor behavior—and ask what the data says.
